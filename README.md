# PROJECT: AgriDeep.tn — Premium GeoAI Landing Page

## 1. Product and business context

Design a high-quality, responsive landing page for AgriDeep, an early-stage Tunisian GeoAI startup exploring how satellite imagery and geospatial intelligence can improve visibility into olive grove conditions throughout the growing season.

The target audience is:
- Olive oil producers and exporters in Tunisia.
- International olive oil importers and distributors.
- Bottlers and premium olive oil brands interested in documented provenance.

The website's primary business goal is to turn visitors into discovery-call bookings and potential pilot partners.

IMPORTANT: AgriDeep is currently validating its concept. Do not present it as a fully operational product. Clearly communicate that the company is seeking industry feedback and initial pilot partners.

## 2. Art direction

Create a premium, minimal, modern scientific brand identity.

The design should combine:
- The elegance of a premium Mediterranean agricultural brand.
- The precision of a geospatial intelligence company.
- The credibility of a modern B2B SaaS startup.

Avoid generic agricultural templates, excessive gradients, cartoon illustrations, busy dashboards, stock-photo-heavy layouts and traditional corporate blue.

### Color palette
- Deep forest green: #17251C
- Olive green: #68794B
- Warm off-white: #F6F5EF
- Muted sage: #DDE3D5
- Charcoal text: #252B25
- White: #FFFFFF

Use generous whitespace, strong typography, subtle borders, elegant spacing and restrained animation.

Use a refined sans-serif typeface such as Inter or Manrope. Headlines should feel confident, concise and editorial.

## 3. Header

Create a clean navigation bar with:
- AgriDeep wordmark on the left.
- How It Works
- Who It's For
- About
- A prominent button: "Discuss a Pilot"

Keep the header compact and responsive, with a mobile menu on smaller screens.

Do not invent an existing logo. Create a simple wordmark treatment and, optionally, a subtle geometric symbol inspired by an olive leaf and satellite orbit.

## 4. Hero section

Use a visually striking, wide hero composition.

Visual direction:
A high-quality aerial photograph of a Tunisian or Mediterranean olive grove, ideally with olive tree patterns visible from above. Overlay a restrained, scientifically inspired geospatial element such as a subtle parcel boundary, coordinate grid or vegetation-monitoring layer.

Do not make the image look like a fictional live satellite product interface.

Small eyebrow text:
"SATELLITE INTELLIGENCE FOR OLIVE GROVES"

Main headline:
"Know the grove behind every drop."

Supporting text:
"AgriDeep is exploring how satellite intelligence can help olive oil businesses monitor orchard conditions over time and build a richer picture of the origin behind their products."

Primary CTA:
"Discuss a Pilot"

Secondary CTA:
"How It Works"

Below the CTAs, add a small transparent status label:
"Early-stage initiative · Seeking pilot partners"

The hero must immediately communicate what AgriDeep does and why an olive oil business should care.

## 5. Problem section

Headline:
"Origin tells a story. The orchard tells you more."

Explain the problem without making unsupported industry-wide claims.

Use three concise content blocks:

A. Limited visibility
"Traditional product and traceability documents may not capture how orchard conditions evolve during the growing season."

B. Fragmented information
"Producers, exporters and buyers may rely on different records to understand the origin and production context of olive oil."

C. Communicating provenance
"Businesses need credible, understandable evidence to communicate the story behind their products to buyers."

Use minimal icons or subtle visual diagrams. Avoid dramatic red warning graphics.

## 6. How it works

Headline:
"From satellite observations to orchard-level insight."

Present a clear three-step horizontal process on desktop and vertical process on mobile.

Step 1 — Observe
"Use satellite imagery to observe vegetation conditions across a selected olive grove over time."

Step 2 — Analyze
"Calculate indicators such as NDVI to explore vegetation patterns and changes between usable observations."

Step 3 — Document
"Organize observations, dates, maps and limitations into an understandable orchard-monitoring record."

Use elegant visual cards, a simple process line and restrained geospatial illustrations.

Clearly state that satellite observations complement, rather than replace, field inspections, traceability records and laboratory testing.

## 7. Illustrative monitoring preview

Create a polished visual preview that communicates the intended product experience.

Include:
- A simplified olive grove parcel map.
- A clearly labeled illustrative vegetation-index layer.
- A simple NDVI trend chart with dates.
- An observation timeline.
- A data-quality note explaining that cloud cover and image availability can affect results.

IMPORTANT:
This is a conceptual mockup, not a functioning analytics product. Label the section "Illustrative product concept" and the chart "Illustrative data — not live satellite measurements."

Do not fabricate customer data, verified results, real satellite analysis, accuracy percentages, environmental certifications or performance claims.

The preview should feel credible and restrained, not like an overly complex enterprise dashboard.

## 8. Who it's for

Create three elegant audience cards.

Olive oil producers and exporters:
"Document orchard conditions and add useful context to existing origin and traceability records."

Importers and distributors:
"Explore additional information that may help evaluate suppliers and communicate product provenance."

Bottlers and premium brands:
"Explore new ways to tell a more transparent story about where olives were grown."

Do not promise higher prices, guaranteed sales, verified authenticity or improved oil quality.

## 9. Pilot partnership section

Use a contrasting deep-green background.

Headline:
"Help shape the future of olive oil provenance."

Body:
"AgriDeep is speaking with olive oil professionals to understand their challenges before building the product. We are looking for a small number of producers, exporters and importers willing to share their experience and explore an initial pilot."

Primary CTA:
"Become a Pilot Partner"

Supporting text:
"An initial discovery conversation is exploratory and does not commit either party to a commercial agreement."

## 10. Contact form

Design a simple, professional form with:
- Full name
- Business email
- Company
- Role: Producer / Exporter / Importer / Distributor / Bottler / Other
- Optional message

Submit button:
"Request a Conversation"

Include a success state:
"Thank you for your interest in AgriDeep. We'll be in touch to explore whether a conversation or pilot could be useful."

Do not imply that form submissions are already connected to a working backend. Design the form UI so it can be connected during implementation.

## 11. Footer

Include:
- AgriDeep wordmark.
- "Satellite intelligence for olive grove monitoring."
- Navigation links.
- Contact email placeholder: [ADD OFFICIAL AGRIDEEP EMAIL]
- Copyright notice.

Do not invent an email address, phone number, office address, social account or company registration number.

## 12. UX and implementation requirements

- Design the full desktop landing page first, then create a corresponding mobile layout.
- Use a responsive grid and readable mobile typography.
- Make primary CTA buttons visually prominent and consistent.
- Ensure strong contrast and accessible form labels.
- Use realistic olive grove imagery and simple scientific data visualizations.
- Keep the page focused on a single conversion goal: booking discovery conversations and identifying pilot partners.
- Avoid login pages, pricing tables, admin panels, unnecessary navigation and complex app features.
- Keep sections modular so the design can later be implemented in React or Next.js.
- Make the final result feel like a credible, premium, early-stage geospatial intelligence startup.

Deliver a complete, cohesive, high-fidelity landing page design, not merely a wireframe.
